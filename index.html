• AI ad vs real creative

Same brief. Two very different results.

AI-generatedFrom a text prompt
  • The two photographers don't exist. They aren't anyone's team
  • It redesigned the client's bags, logo and candy, so it isn't their real product
  • Small print turns to gibberish: “by Sweden” on the bags is unreadable
  • The “Swedish” box has a white cross, not Sweden's yellow one
Made by our teamShot, designed and written by people
  • Real people from our team, photographed by us
  • Type, colour and layout built for the brand
  • Every word written and checked by a person
  • Planned around who you want to reach

Each AI version was made from the same brief as the real ad beside it. We used AI only to show the difference.
